Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 24, 2026 • Effective Date: November 24, 2026

At RapidClip (LiveClip), accessible from our web application, your privacy is one of our top priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected, how it is used, and how your ephemeral clipboard data is handled.

Quick Summary: RapidClip requires no user registration or accounts. Clipboard text, images, and files shared within rooms are temporary and auto-deleted after approximately 48 hours.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We believe in privacy by design. We do not require or collect:

  • Personal names, email addresses, or phone numbers.
  • Passwords or account credentials.
  • Payment information or credit card numbers.

2. Ephemeral Clipboard Data & Storage

When you create or join a room, any text, images, or files you upload are stored temporarily via Google Firebase Realtime Database to facilitate real-time synchronization between your connected devices:

  • Data Retention: All room contents (text, images, and uploaded files) are designed to be temporary and are automatically deleted after approximately 48 hours.
  • Room Visibility: Any user who possesses or correctly enters your room ID can read the active text and files in that room. For confidential information, we recommend using long, randomized room identifiers.

3. Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage (localStorage) strictly for enhancing user experience:

  • lc_th: Remembers your preferred interface theme (Dark or Light).
  • lc_recent: Saves up to 5 recently visited room IDs on your device for fast re-entry.
  • lc_consent: Stores your cookie preference choices.

This data stays exclusively on your device and is never transmitted to any third-party marketing servers.

4. Google AdSense & Third-Party Cookies

We display advertisements powered by Google AdSense to keep RapidClip 100% free for everyone. Google and its advertising partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website and other sites across the Internet:

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

5. European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

For visitors residing within the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom:

  • You have the right to request access to, rectification of, or erasure of any ephemeral data associated with your room identifier.
  • Because RapidClip maintains zero user accounts or identifiable profiles, manual deletion can be triggered immediately inside any room using the in-room delete controls or leaving the room to let the 48-hour auto-purge take effect.

6. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)

Under the CCPA, California consumers have the right to request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information collected, and the right to request that a business not sell their personal information. RapidClip does not sell any user data.
