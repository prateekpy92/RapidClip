About RapidClip & Usage Guides

Created by Prateek • Fast, No-Login Cross-Device Sharing

RapidClip (LiveClip) was created to eliminate one of the most common friction points in modern computing: getting a snippet of text, an image, or a file from your phone to your PC (or vice versa) in seconds without signing into bloated apps or emailing yourself.

How It Works Under the Hood

RapidClip creates temporary, secure channels called Rooms powered by Google Firebase Realtime Database. When two or more devices enter the same room identifier:

  • Instant WebSockets: A live WebSocket connection synchronizes any typed text across all devices with zero perceptible lag.
  • Smart Image Downscaling: Images are compressed client-side to prevent cellular bandwidth waste while preserving visual clarity.
  • Chunked Binary Delivery: Files up to 50 MB are split into 2 MB chunks and reconstructed as clean downloadable Blobs on the receiving device.
  • Automatic Lifecycle: All data automatically expires and deletes after approximately 48 hours.

Step-by-Step Guides

1How to Copy Text from Phone to PC

  1. Open RapidClip on your PC and click Generate random room (or enter a custom name).
  2. Click the Share / QR button in the top navigation bar.
  3. Point your smartphone's camera at the QR code and tap the link to open the room on your phone.
  4. Paste or type your text on your phone — it will appear instantly on your PC monitor!
  5. Click Copy on your PC to paste it into your documents or code editor.

2How to Send Photos Without Quality Loss or Login

  1. Join the same room on both your mobile device and computer.
  2. Tap the Images tab on your phone and choose a photo from your gallery or snap a new shot.
  3. The image is optimized and uploaded in milliseconds.
  4. On your computer, click the image card in the room gallery to view it full-size in the lightbox, or tap download to save it directly.

3How to Transfer Large Files (Up to 50 MB)

  1. Click the Files tab in your room.
  2. Drag and drop any document, PDF, ZIP archive, or media file up to 50 MB.
  3. The file is chunked and streamed across the database.
  4. The receiving device shows the file name, size, and download button. Tap the download icon to save it locally.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to install any app or browser extension?

No! RapidClip runs entirely in standard web browsers (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge). You can also add it to your home screen as a Progressive Web App (PWA) for 1-tap access.

Can anyone else see my room?

Rooms are private by obscurity — only people who enter the exact room name can view its contents. For sensitive items, use our random 6-character room generator or a long alphanumeric passphrase.

How long is my data stored?

All room contents are strictly temporary and are purged after approximately 48 hours. You can also manually delete images or files anytime using the in-room trash icons.
