Frequently asked questions

What is RapidClip (LiveClip)?

RapidClip is a free, instant cross-device clipboard. Open a room on one device, scan the QR code on another, and text snippets, images, and files appear on both in real time without login.

How do I move text from my phone to my PC?

Create a room on your PC, then tap the Share button inside the room to display a QR code. Scan it with your phone to sync instantly.

Is my data private?

Anyone who possesses the room code can read its contents. For sensitive clips, use our random room generator and clear your room when done.

How long are files kept?

Files, history clips, and images are temporary and removed after about 48 hours. Files can be up to 50 MB each.
